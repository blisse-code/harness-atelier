# Handovers

One entry per substantive session, newest last, written by the `handover` skill. The next session reads the newest entry first and mirrors it back. Each entry is a level-3 heading with the date and the person, then five short labelled lines:

- **Did:** what happened, in a sentence or two
- **Committed:** decisions made (also in `docs/decisions.md`)
- **Sketching:** explored but not decided
- **Open:** questions and blockers, with who can answer
- **Next:** the first thing the next session should do, and what it can do without asking
